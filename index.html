My engineering journey

I am a Master’s student in Wind Energy Engineering at Hochschule Flensburg with a prior Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering. My passion lies at the intersection of automation, structural dynamics, control, aeroelastic simulations, and wind farm optimisation.

During my Master’s projects, I performed aeroelastic load simulations for a 20 MW offshore monopile wind turbine using OpenFAST, Python, and MATLAB, including IEC 61400‑1 Design Load Cases. Beyond simulations, I developed Python automation to streamline OpenFAST workflows and cut manual post‑processing time.

I also bring professional experience from Paydar (solar energy systems) and TehranRigzar (mining equipment), where I led structural analyses (ANSYS), technical documentation, and cross‑functional project coordination. Earlier, I developed a control system for an underwater welding robot (MATLAB) and contributed as a volunteer engineer to an artificial hand prosthesis project.
